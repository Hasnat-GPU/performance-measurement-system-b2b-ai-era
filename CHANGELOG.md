## [1.0.0] — 2026-08-07

Initial design.

### Added

- **Framing (§1–2).** The four-jobs conflict as the root cause of performance-system
  failure; rater-variance, Goodhart, unit-of-analysis and resulting as the four
  measurement problems the design must survive.
- **Field survey (§3).** Google, Amazon, Netflix, Apple, Meta, Microsoft and
  Deloitte/Adobe, each with mechanism, strengths, weaknesses and an explicit
  adopt/reject transfer decision.
- **Design principles (§4).** Thirteen principles, each stated with its derivation
  so it can be argued with rather than obeyed.
- **Architecture (§5).** Four decoupled layers — Sensing, Judgment, Consequence, Meta.
- **Team assessment method (§6).** Five team dimensions; three individual axes;
  Contribution Ledger; Signal Pulses with a counterfactual item; rater
  fingerprinting and standardization; confidence-rated bands and the
  Low-Confidence Rule; the System Attribution Gate.
- **Strategic elevation plan (§7).** Ambiguity-based ladder; two-context promotion
  rule; the Scope Obligation; Capability Map with maturity levels; Bench Depth
  Ratio; Strategy Delta Review; Talent Export Credit; the inverted transfer bar.
- **Recognition and reward system (§8).** Four currencies; the Re-recruitment
  Review replacing the Keeper Test; non-convertible peer recognition;
  Opportunity Share auditing; the Prevention Credit.
- **Governance (§9–14).** Twelve differentiators; Layer 4 meta-metrics; failure-mode
  table with detectors; ten explicit refusals; cost model and scaling; rollout
  roadmap with stated kill criteria; honest limitations.
